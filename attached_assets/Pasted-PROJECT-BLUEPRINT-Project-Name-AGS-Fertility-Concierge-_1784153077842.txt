PROJECT BLUEPRINT

Project Name

AGS Fertility Concierge

(Tagline can evolve later.)

⸻

Mission

Provide Canadian intended parents with a trusted, transparent, and compassionate way to explore fertility treatment with carefully selected IVF hospitals in Bangalore, while supporting them through coordination, travel planning, and ongoing communication.

The business facilitates access and logistics. Medical care is provided by licensed fertility specialists and hospitals.

⸻

Brand Personality

The website should feel like:

* Calm
* Hopeful
* Honest
* Professional
* Private
* Caring
* Premium
* Modern

Avoid:

* Salesy language
* Countdown timers
* Flashing banners
* Aggressive popups
* “Guaranteed success”
* Price-first marketing

Visitors should leave thinking:

“These people understand what we’re going through.”

Website Goals

The website should answer, within the first minute:

* Can I trust this company?
* Are the hospitals reputable?
* What does the process look like?
* What are the approximate costs?
* Who helps me?
* What happens after I return home?

Only then ask them to book a consultation.

About Us

* What AGS does and does not do

⸻

IVF in Bangalore

Cover:

* Why Bangalore
* Medical ecosystem
* Technology
* Experienced specialists
* International patient support
* Typical treatment journey
* Travel considerations

⸻

Treatments

Individual pages for:

* IVF
* ICSI
* Egg Freezing
* Embryo Freezing
* Donor Programs
* Fertility Preservation

Each page should explain:

* What it is
* Who it may help
* Typical process
* Questions to discuss with a specialist
* Recovery
* FAQs

Avoid giving personalized medical advice.

⸻

Partner Hospitals

Each profile should include:

* Hospital overview
* Accreditations
* Areas of expertise
* Facilities
* International patient services
* Languages
* Approximate treatment cost ranges (with a note that individual cases vary)
* Map
* Photos
* Consultation request button

⸻

Cost Guide

Instead of promising exact prices:

Provide estimated ranges.

Include:

* Treatment
* Medication
* Flights
* Accommodation
* Local transportation
* Miscellaneous

Offer sample budgets (Economy, Standard, Premium) with clear disclaimers.

⸻

Success Stories

Feature real stories only when permission is obtained.

Include:

* Journey summary
* Challenges
* Support received
* Outcome (without implying similar results for others)

⸻
