## 1. Workforce Categories

AGS operates **11 workforce categories**, one per business unit. Each workforce:

- Has defined responsibilities within its domain
- Has defined approval authority levels
- Is compatible with WEF v1.1 (enterprise execution framework)
- Is compatible with PSER (project state & execution registry)
- Maps to future platform capabilities
